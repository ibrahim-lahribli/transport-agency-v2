## 3. Practicalities (checked 2026-09-30)

- **Fixed hours:** no fixed opening hours for the Taroudant ramparts, medina or Tiout oasis; souks are daytime and generally open every day. Tiout kasbah and oasis access are informal.
- **Entrance fees (research only):** none reliably documented for the walls, medina or oasis; a small local fee for the donkey ride is typical. Not published on the website.
- **Seasonality:** the Souss plain is very hot in summer; the morning start and a shaded lunch help. Late autumn to spring is the most comfortable window.
- **Argan goats:** the classic sightings are on the coastal Agadir–Essaouira corridor. Argan does grow inland around the Souss and Anti-Atlas, but goats in trees on the Taroudant road are **much less reliably reported**. Treat as a possible, unguaranteed sight only.
- **Effort:** full day, about 3.5 to 4.5 hours in the vehicle, plus walking in Taroudant and the oasis (flat, sandy palm groves).
- **Safety norms (typical industry practice, to be confirmed by our operator):** none specific. The optional donkey ride should be arranged with a local provider and is at the guest's discretion.
- **What to bring:** comfortable shoes, hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, cash for purchases and the donkey ride.
- **Etiquette:** Taroudant is a conservative inland town; modest dress is more comfortable. Ask before photographing people. Bargaining is normal in the souks.
