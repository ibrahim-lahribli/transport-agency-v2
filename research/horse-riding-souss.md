## 3. Practicalities (checked 2026-09-30)

- **Fixed hours:** none; rides are arranged on request, morning or late afternoon.
- **Entrance fees (research only):** none. Not applicable.
- **Seasonality:** late-afternoon rides are cooler in summer; mornings are better in the hotter months. The Souss plain is hot and dry in July and August.
- **Effort:** low to moderate; a walking or trotting ride on flat ground suits beginners.
- **Safety norms (typical industry practice, to be confirmed by our operator):** a helmet is normally offered; riders should state their experience level honestly; there is usually a **weight limit** and a **minimum age**, and children are often led or accompanied. Riding is not recommended in pregnancy or with back problems. Not to be stated as our policy.
- **What to bring:** long trousers, closed shoes, sunscreen and hat, water.
- **Etiquette:** approach horses calmly; ask before photographing riders and handlers; do not feed the horses without asking.
