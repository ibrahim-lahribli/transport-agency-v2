## 3. Practicalities (checked 2026-09-30)

| Site | Days | Hours | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crocoparc Agadir | 7 days a week, 365 days a year | 10:00–18:00 | Official site; one third-party blog cites a 09:30 open — treat the official 10:00 as correct |

- **Entrance fees (research only):** the official site lists adult and child prices (reported around 100 MAD adult, 70 MAD child 3–12; other listings quote 90 MAD). Prices change, so treat as unverified and **never publish** on the website.
- **Park description check:** accurate. It is a botanical garden and wildlife park known for Nile crocodiles (reportedly 300+), giant tortoises, and other reptiles, with planted gardens over a few hectares.
- **Seasonality:** the park is open all year; it is outdoors and hot at midday in summer. Mornings are more comfortable.
- **Effort:** low. Walking on paved paths; generally family-friendly and manageable with young children.
- **Safety norms (typical industry practice, to be confirmed by our operator):** stay behind the barriers and follow park signage. No protective gear needed.
- **What to bring:** hat and sunscreen, comfortable shoes, cash or card for tickets, water.
- **Etiquette:** keep noise down near the animals; follow park rules on feeding.
