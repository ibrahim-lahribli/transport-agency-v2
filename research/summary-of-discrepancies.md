## Cross-cutting notes

- **Schema normalisation:** four draft files use a string for `durationHours` (`timlalin-dunes`, `quad-buggy-forest`, `horse-riding-souss`, `crocoparc`). The patch schema requires a number, so all patches emit a number and carry the range in the copy.
- **Wording substitutions applied in every patch:** "guide"/"professional guide" → "driver"/"host"; "book now" → "request"/"check availability"; no prices or entrance fees in published copy (fees are recorded only in `research/<id>.md` and date-stamped).
- **Never-use list enforced:** no "Sahara" for the Massa/Tiznit dunes (and the denial sentence is used where helpful); no promised swimming, goats or birds; no "authentic", "best" or ratings claims in published copy.
- **"Typical industry practice, to be confirmed by our operator"** is used for all quad, buggy, horse, camel and boat safety norms, and is not stated as our policy.
