# boat-cruise — research notes

- Product id: `boat-cruise`
- Researched: 2026-09-30
- Start point: central Agadir, Boulevard du 20 Août area
- Verdict: the draft timings are realistic. Operators vary between about 4 and 6 hours; the draft's 4.75 h sits in the middle.

## 1. Verified route

The "route" is a short road transfer to the Marina plus a sea cruise in the Bay of Agadir.

| # | From → To | km | min (range) | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Central Agadir → Agadir Marina | 4 | 10–15 | map estimate | likely |
| 2 | Agadir Marina → coastal cruise in the bay (sea leg, no road) | — | up to 2 h at sea | operator listings | likely |
| 3 | Agadir Marina → hotels (return) | 4 | 10–15 | map estimate | likely |

- **Total round trip (road):** ~8 km
- **Total road driving:** ~20 to 30 min
- **Sea portion:** a cruise along the bay with a fishing stop and a swim stop
- **Stop order check:** the catalogue order (marina → cruise → fishing → swim → BBQ → marina) is logical.
- **Meeting point:** operators commonly meet at the Agadir Marina near the restaurant row (one listing names the spot opposite the "Pure Passion" restaurant). Exact berth to be confirmed.

- **Recommended:** `durationHours` 4.75, `pickupWindow` "08:00 to 08:45", `returnApprox` "14:00 to 14:45".

## 3. Practicalities (checked 2026-09-30)

- **Fixed hours:** none; departures depend on the boat and the weather.
- **Entrance fees (research only):** none. Not applicable.
- **Seasonality:** winter swells can shorten or cancel trips; the bay is often calmer in the morning. Summer is calmer at sea but hot on land. Sea state is the main variable.
- **Effort:** low. Sitting on a boat, with a swim stop for those who want it.
- **Safety norms (typical industry practice, to be confirmed by our operator):** life jackets and a safety briefing on boarding; the crew advises on the swim stop; guests who cannot swim should stay aboard or use a float. Not to be stated as our policy.
- **What to bring:** swimwear and towel, sunscreen, hat and sunglasses, a light jacket for the water, motion-sickness tablets if prone.
- **Etiquette:** none specific; follow the crew's instructions on board.
