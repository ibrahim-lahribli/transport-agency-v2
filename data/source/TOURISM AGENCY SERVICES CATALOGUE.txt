# TOURISM AGENCY SERVICES CATALOGUE

## 1.1 Paradise Valley – Nature & Authentic Moroccan Experience

**Duration:** Full-day excursion
**Departure:** Agadir
**Experience type:** Nature • Culture • Local traditions • Relaxation

Discover Paradise Valley, one of the most beautiful natural areas near Agadir, combining mountain landscapes, palm trees, natural pools, and authentic Berber villages.

### Highlights

**Argan Oil Cooperative**

* Visit a local argan oil cooperative.
* Learn about traditional argan oil production.
* Taste authentic argan oil and Amlou.
* Discover locally produced products.

**Traditional Pottery Workshop**

* Meet a local artisan.
* Discover traditional Moroccan pottery techniques.
* Watch a demonstration of the pottery wheel.
* Enjoy a photo opportunity with traditional pieces.

**Panoramic Viewpoint**

* Stop at a scenic viewpoint overlooking the surrounding valleys and argan trees.
* Enjoy free time for photographs.

**Paradise Valley**

* Walk through the natural landscape along the river.
* Discover the famous natural pools.
* Relax in the peaceful surroundings.
* Swimming may be possible depending on seasonal water conditions.
* Optional traditional Moroccan Tajine may be available locally.

### Included

* Air-conditioned transportation.
* Transportation from/to the designated pickup point.
* Scenic and photo stops.

### Recommended

* Comfortable walking shoes.
* Swimsuit if you plan to swim.
* Hat, sunscreen, and water.

**Departure:** Agadir
**Experience type:** Nature • Culture • Coast • Desert • Local cuisine

Discover the diversity of southern Morocco in one day, from the Atlantic coast and Souss-Massa landscapes to the traditional city of Tiznit and nearby sand dunes.

* Discover traditional Moroccan pottery.
* Meet local artisans and learn about their craftsmanship.

**Atlantic Coast**

**Tiznit**

* Explore the historic city of Tiznit.

**Sand Dunes**

**Culture, nature, coast, and adventure combined in one day.**

## 1.4 Essaouira – Mogador Coastal Day Trip

**Duration:** Full day
**Departure:** Agadir
**Experience type:** History • Culture • Coast • Medina • Shopping

Discover Essaouira, the historic Atlantic port city also known as Mogador, famous for its fortified medina, fishing harbor, artistic atmosphere, and Atlantic coastline.

### Departure

* Pickup from your hotel or residence in Agadir.
* Suggested departure: around 7:30 AM.

### Along the Coast

**Tamri Panoramic Stop**

* Stop at a scenic viewpoint overlooking the Atlantic coastline and Tamri area.

**Argan Tree Goats**

* Depending on season and local conditions, you may see goats climbing argan trees.
* This is a spontaneous wildlife/farming sight rather than a guaranteed stop.

**Argan Oil Cooperative**

* Visit a local cooperative.
* Discover traditional argan oil production and local products.

### Essaouira

Explore the historic city and discover:

* The fortified Medina.
* The historic city walls.
* The harbor and fishing port.
* Skala/Scala fortifications.
* The traditional souks.
* Historic cannons and coastal defenses.
* The picturesque streets and Atlantic atmosphere.

### Free Time

Enjoy time to:

* Explore the Medina.
* Have lunch.
* Visit local shops and galleries.
* Discover the harbor.
* Relax by the Atlantic Ocean.

### Important Note

Essaouira is a historic Moroccan coastal city and former port of international importance. It is best presented on the website as a **historic Atlantic city / Mogador**, rather than as one of Morocco's imperial cities.
